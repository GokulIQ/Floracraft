Four Seasons Botanical Guide
Spring (Active Growth & Repotting)

Best window to size up planters by 2 inches, replace tired soil, and resume organic liquid feedings.

Summer (Peak Transpiration & Misting)

Soil dries twice as quickly. Check soil twice weekly and mist tropical broad leaves in the early morning.

Autumn (Pre-Dormancy Prep)

Gradually reduce fertilizer dosages and wipe down leaves to maximize diminished daylight absorption.

Winter (Rest & Drought Protection)

Water sparingly. Move plants away from cold drafty windows and hot radiator forced-air currents.
