Growing Happiness, One Plant & Handmade Pot at a Time

We believe connecting humans with living flora elevates mental clarity, purifies residential spaces, and fosters environmental reverence.

The FloraCraft Journey

From a Small Family Greenhouse to a Nationwide Botanical Collective

Founded in 2014 by master horticulturist Dr. Alistair Finch, FloraCraft began with a simple frustration: big-box stores were selling root-bound, pesticide-soaked plants in plastic containers that withered within weeks.

We pioneered an organic nursery standard: propagating resilient, pest-resistant tropical and perennial specimens potted exclusively in breathable Italian terracotta and porous Nordic ceramic glazes with bio-active mycorrhizal soils.
